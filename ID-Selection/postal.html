Postal ID

The Postal ID is the primary identification card issued by the Philippine Postal Corporation (PHLPost). It is recognized by all government agencies and financial institutions as a valid proof of identity and address.

Application Process

1

Fill out application form

Complete two (2) copies of the Unified Postal ID application form. Ensure all information is accurate and matches your supporting documents.

2

Prepare requirements

Gather all necessary documents including proof of identity and proof of address. Photocopies should be prepared alongside original copies for verification.

3

Visit Postal ID office/post office

Locate the nearest PHLPost branch or specialized Postal ID capturing center in your municipality or at designated malls.

4

Submit requirements

Present your documents to the counter officer for evaluation and pay the required processing fee.

5

Biometrics/photo-capture

Proceed to the capturing station for your digital photograph, fingerprints, and digital signature.

6

Wait for ID release

Your Postal ID will be delivered to your registered address via mail. Delivery timelines vary by region (approx. 15-30 working days).

inventory_2 Mandatory Requirements

  • check_circle PSA Birth Certificate
  • check_circle Proof of Address (e.g., Utility Bill)
  • check_circle Filled Postal ID application form

Quick Tip

You can find ID capturing centers in most major Robinson's and SM Malls nationwide for faster processing.
