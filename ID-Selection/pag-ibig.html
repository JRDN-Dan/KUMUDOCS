Pag-IBIG Membership & ID

The Postal ID is the primary identification card issued by the Philippine Postal Corporation (PHLPost). It is recognized by all government agencies and financial institutions as a valid proof of identity and address.

how_to_reg Application Process

1

Register Online or In-Person

Visit the official Pag-IBIG online registration portal or proceed to the nearest HDMF branch to initiate your membership application.

2

Fill out Member Data Form (MDF)

Complete the Member Data Form with accurate personal details, employment history, and beneficiary information. Ensure all fields are legible if filing manually.

3

Submit Requirements

Present your original documents and photocopies to the Pag-IBIG representative for verification and processing of your membership record.

4

Receive Pag-IBIG MID Number

Once verified, you will be issued a 12-digit Pag-IBIG Membership ID (MID) number, which serves as your permanent reference for all fund transactions.

5

Optional Loyalty Card Application

You may opt to apply for the Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card Plus, which doubles as a transaction card and provides discounts at partner establishments nationwide.

language Official Portal

Access the official Pag-IBIG Virtual portal for online registration, contributions, and loan applications.

Mandatory Requirements

Document Checklist

  • check_circle
    Valid ID Government-issued identification (Passport, Driver's License, PRC ID, etc.)
  • check_circle
    PSA Birth Certificate Original and clear photocopy of your Birth Certificate from the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  • check_circle
    Member Data Form (MDF) Fully accomplished form with no erasures. Available at branches or online.

* Note: Additional documents may be required for special membership categories (OFW, Voluntary, or Self-Employed).
