PhilHealth ID

Your gateway to social health insurance in the Philippines. Essential for hospitalizations and medical subsidies.

assignment

Application Process

1

Download and fill out PMRF form

The PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF) is the primary document required for registration.

2

Prepare requirements

Ensure all original documents and photocopies are ready for verification.

3

Visit nearest PhilHealth office

Go to any Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) or PhilHealth Express outlet.

4

Submit documents

Hand over your accomplished PMRF and supporting documents to the officer.

5

Receive PhilHealth Number

Your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) will be generated and issued immediately.

6

Wait for ID or MDR issuance

The PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record (MDR) will be printed for you.
