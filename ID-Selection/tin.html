Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)

A permanent identification number issued by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) to every taxpayer in the Philippines for tax purposes, identification, and government transactions.

open_in_new Visit BIR official website

account_tree Registration Process

1

Determine applicable BIR form

Identify your taxpayer category (e.g., employee, self-employed, or estate) to select the correct registration form. Most individuals use Form 1902 or 1904.

2

Fill out form

Complete the BIR registration form with accurate personal information. Ensure all mandatory fields are legible and match your official identification documents.

3

Submit requirements to BIR office

Visit the Revenue District Office (RDO) having jurisdiction over your place of residence or business address to submit your documents.

4

Receive TIN Number

Once processed, you will be issued your unique Taxpayer Identification Number. Keep this number secure as it is permanent for life.

5
Optional

TIN ID application

You may request the physical TIN ID card after your number is generated. Check with your RDO for availability and specific ID issuance schedules.
