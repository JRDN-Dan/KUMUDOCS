SSS ID & Number Application

The Social Security System (SSS) provides a permanent identification number for all members. This guide outlines the institutional process for securing your SS Number and the subsequent UMID/SSS ID card.

account_tree

Application Steps

1

Create SSS online account

Access the My.SSS portal to initiate your registration. This serves as your digital gateway for all future SSS transactions.

2

Apply for SS Number

Complete the online application form with accurate personal details. Ensure your name matches your primary birth documents exactly.

3

Upload or submit requirements

Digital copies of your PSA Birth Certificate and valid identification must be uploaded or presented at the nearest SSS branch.

4

Receive SS Number

Once verified, you will receive your permanent SS Number via email or through the online portal notification system.

5

Apply for UMID/SSS ID

Optional

Members with at least one posted contribution may proceed to biometric capture for the Unified Multi-Purpose ID (UMID).

Mandatory Requirements

  • check_circle

    PSA Birth Certificate

    Original or certified true copy from the Philippine Statistics Authority.

  • check_circle

    Valid ID

    Passport, Driver's License, or PRC Card (if birth certificate is unavailable).

  • check_circle

    SS Number application form

    Duly accomplished Form E-1 or the online summary of registration.

info Note

Ensure all scanned documents are clear and legible. Discrepancies between your ID and application form may result in processing delays.
