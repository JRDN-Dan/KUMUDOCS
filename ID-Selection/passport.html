Passport Application

Philippine citizens can choose to apply for passports in the Philippines from any of our regional consular offices, satellite offices located in selected malls.

format_list_numbered

Steps:

  1. 1

    Online Appointment

    Secure an appointment through the official DFA Online Passport Appointment System (OPAS).

  2. 2

    Fill out Application Form

    Complete the application form provided during the online booking process.

  3. 3

    Pay Processing Fee

    Settle the passport fee through authorized payment centers to confirm your schedule.

  4. 4

    Visit DFA Site

    Appear personally at the DFA office on your scheduled date for biometrics and data capturing.

  5. 5

    Passport Delivery/Pick-up

    Choose between courier delivery or manual pick-up once the passport is released.

assignment_turned_in

Requirements:

  • check_circle

    Accomplished Application Form (Printed from OPAS)

  • check_circle

    PSA Certified Birth Certificate (Original & Photocopy)

  • check_circle

    Any valid Government-issued ID (Original & Photocopy)

  • check_circle

    Proof of Appointment & Payment Receipt

  • check_circle

    For married women: PSA Marriage Contract

info Note:

Personal appearance is required for all applicants. Please wear appropriate attire (collared shirts) for the photograph capturing.
