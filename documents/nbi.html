NBI Clearance

The National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance is a government-issued document confirming that an individual has no criminal record in the Philippines.

format_list_numbered Application Process

1

Register online

Create an account on the official NBI Clearance website and fill out the application form accurately.

2

Set appointment

Select your preferred NBI Branch, date, and time slot (AM or PM) for your personal appearance.

3

Pay fee

Pay the clearance fee through authorized payment channels (7-Eleven, GCash, Maya, or Bank Transfer) using your reference number.

4

Visit branch

Go to your chosen NBI branch on your scheduled date. Present your printed application form or reference number.

5

Biometrics

Have your photo, digital signature, and fingerprints captured by the NBI personnel.

6

Receive clearance

If you have no "HIT," your clearance will be printed immediately. If there is a "HIT," follow instructions for the clearance release date.

language Official Portal

Access the official NBI website for online appointment scheduling.

assignment Requirements

  • check_circle
    Valid IDs Two (2) valid government-issued IDs (e.g., Passport, Driver's License, UMID, PhilID).
  • check_circle
    Online account A registered account on the NBI Clearance portal with completed personal information.
  • check_circle
    Reference number Your unique 10-digit NBI reference number and the official payment receipt.
info

Note: Dress code for personal appearance is strictly observed. Please wear semi-formal or decent attire. Wearing of shorts, slippers, and sleeveless shirts is prohibited during the biometric capture.
