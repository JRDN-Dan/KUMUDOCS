Birth Certificate

Official document issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) certifying the details of a person's birth.

how_to_reg Application Process

1

Request online or visit PSA outlet

You can choose to apply through the official PSA Serbilis website or walk into any PSA Civil Registry System (CRS) outlet nationwide.

open_in_new Visit PSA's official website location_on Find Nearest PSA Outlet
2

Fill request form

Complete the Birth Certificate Application Form (AF) with accurate details including full name, parents' names, and place of birth.

3

Pay processing fee

Settle the required fees at the payment counter or through authorized online payment channels. Prices may vary between walk-in and online delivery.

4

Wait for release/delivery

If walk-in, wait for your document to be printed. For online requests, the certificate will be delivered to your registered address within the specified timeframe.

assignment Requirements
